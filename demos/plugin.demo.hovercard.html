. Each hover card is a separate, independent plugin instance.
Each [data-plugin-hovercard] is a self-contained instance. Any number can coexist in prose without conflict.

Four sides, one rule.

Cards open on any side and auto-flip when the viewport doesn't have enough room. The exit animation always runs toward the trigger.

Side Placement Auto-flip enabled
top ↑
side: "top"

Slides up into view. Flips to bottom if there's not enough room above.
left ←
side: "left"

Slides in from the right. Flips to right if no room on the left.
right →
side: "right"

Slides in from the left. Flips to left if no room on the right.
bottom ↓
side: "bottom"

Slides down into view. The default. Flips to top near the bottom of the page.

Dial in the delay.

Open and close delays are independent. Slow open + fast close = glanceable. Fast open + slow close = sticky and explorable.
